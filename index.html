Background

Chris Bither is the Principal of Linekin Advisory. He brings nine years of corporate development experience at LendingTree (2016–2021) and NerdWallet (2021–2025), where he executed approximately $1B in buy-side M&A across ten transactions. In his early roles, he led deal execution end-to-end — sourcing, structuring, diligence, purchase agreement, and integration. Later, he built and managed the teams responsible for that work while owning deal strategy, board materials, and outcomes. Prior to his in-house corporate development career, Chris spent over a decade in financial services — in equity research at UBS, Bank of America, and Dillon Read, in investment banking at Lehman Brothers, and on the buyside at special-situations hedge funds — a multi-lens background that informs how he evaluates, structures, and underwrites acquisitions.
